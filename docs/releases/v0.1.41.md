# CodexAuth Switch v0.1.41

- 统一按套餐分组：Plus → 有五小时限制的 Business → 仅周额度的 Business → 限制不明确的 Business → 其他套餐。
- 无额度记录、缺少重置时间或记录过旧的账号保留套餐优先级；Plus 候选均在 Business 之前尝试，同组未知重置时间的候选排在后面。
- 同组优先使用下一次重置时间更近的账号，重置时间相同再选剩余额度更多的账号。
- 保留低余额跳过、持久耗尽记录、切换后身份与实际额度核验、可取消倒计时等检查；面板说明与文档同步更新。

## English

All candidates now retain their plan priority, including accounts with missing or stale quota records. Plus candidates are attempted before Business. Within each plan group, the nearest upcoming reset takes precedence over remaining balance; unknown reset times sort last within that group. Existing exhaustion, identity, live-quota and countdown checks remain in place.
